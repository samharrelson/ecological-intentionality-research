*September 9, 2026 · Black walnut · 15 minutes*

## Prompt

> Can disciplined attention educate perception such that something previously available only inferentially becomes perceptually salient?

## Field Notes

The leaves are still green, walnuts remain in the branches, and a few more yellow leaves have begun to collect on the ground. Around the exposed roots, grasses and violets have appeared again as the season begins to turn.

*480*

*September 9, 2026. Walnuts among green compound leaves, with sunlight filtering through the canopy.*

I tried first to attend to the walnut as material extension: texture, position, roots, measurable form, and location within this yard, within Spartanburg, and within a watershed. But other features soon became salient: growth, new branches, damage and repair, orientation toward sunlight, relation to a nearby oak, and response to weather and human pruning.

The shift was not from “dead matter” to “life” in a single instant. Rather, a collection of visible properties gradually appeared as the ongoing form of a temporally continuous living being.

## Reflection

I became aware that the practice itself may be educating my perception. What I can now notice as growth, damage, repair, orientation, and responsiveness is not simply the same field of perception I began with years ago.

That does not settle whether “living centeredness” is directly perceived. Repeated observation, inference, conceptual learning, and memory may all be working together. The distinction between perception and inference may therefore be less a matter of choosing one over the other than learning how they cooperate.

The walnut’s otherness has to remain capable of resisting my interpretation. Ecological attention cannot become a permission slip for projection.

## Research Connection

This sit presses on [Ecological Intentionality](../concepts/Ecological%20Intentionality.md) in a useful way. Perhaps ecological intentionality need not name a wholly new intentional structure. It may instead name an **education or formation of intentionality through sustained ecological attention**: a discipline in which features once available mainly through inference become increasingly perceptually salient over time.

That possibility also connects [Attention](../concepts/Attention.md), [Remainder](../concepts/Remainder.md), and [Place and Agency](../concepts/Place%20and%20Agency.md). Increased familiarity need not erase otherness. The question is whether intimacy can deepen while the walnut remains irreducible to the concepts through which I understand it.

## Question Carried Forward

**In the passage from material body to living center, what exactly is perceived, what is inferred, and what is contributed by prior conceptual formation?**
