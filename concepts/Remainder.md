description: Notes on what remains inaccessible within ecological encounter.
---
Remainder names what exceeds any particular act of perception, description, empathy, or interpretation. It is not merely a failure to know. Increasingly, these notes ask whether remainder may be one of the ways genuine otherness becomes positively disclosed.

Remainder also needs to be differentiated by kind. Some absence is horizonal and can become present through another perceptual profile. Some is temporal or epistemic and can be approached through memory, repeated encounter, inference, or scientific knowledge. A stronger remainder would belong to another center in a way that cannot be overcome by accumulating more perspectives or information. The September 23 sit adds an ethical question: whether remainder also requires **care without immunity from correction**: response that remains revisable without pretending that uncertainty itself guarantees adequacy. By September 25, remainder also names something normative: individual, specific-form, and place-wide goods may not line up neatly, and relation itself does not guarantee harmony. September 28 adds a possible **historical and structural remainder**: a power line, fence, or scar can be culturally meaningful within experience while the policy, property relation, decision, or inherited harm shaping it requires another mode of inquiry. September 29 adds physiological processes that are unavailable to unaided perception at this scale but scientifically investigable. October 2 distinguishes personal recollection from a place’s past and from community memory: histories outside one perceiver’s memory may be recoverable through records or held in practices and stories to which that perceiver lacks immediate access. October 3 locates one historical remainder within present form: a trunk can bear consequences of growth and encounter without making their determinate causes transparent. **The past is present enough to matter and absent enough to require inquiry.** October 4 adds a normative remainder within belonging itself: lives can inhabit one field and real relations while their exposures, experiences, and goods remain differentiated. **Belonging need not imply coincidence, beneficence, or one harmonized good.** October 5 asks whether remainder can name a positive differentiation internal to relation rather than only an absence or withheld access. On that proposal, intimacy occurs partly through noncoincidence. These distinctions must not flatten different kinds of absence. Institutional history may be recoverable through archives, biological process through experiment, and another subject’s experience, where experience is warranted, remains non-primordial in a different and stronger sense. **Methodological unavailability ≠ irreducible alterity; personal memory ≠ the measure of a place’s past.**
