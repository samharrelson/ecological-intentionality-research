Franz Brentano enters these Field Notes as a conceptual checkpoint for the word *intentionality*: mental acts are directed toward objects, kinds of act must be distinguished, and an act’s secondary consciousness of itself is not a second act of inner observation.

His role is to make Ecological Intentionality state what changes when *ecological* modifies intentionality. The field practice must first distinguish perceiving, remembering, judging, anticipating, and valuing before asking whether their ecological configuration requires a new term. Brentano’s technical *intentionality* also must not be confused with ordinary purpose, design, biological responsiveness, or agency.

## Works in this project

- *Psychology from an Empirical Standpoint*: the main genealogical checkpoint for intentional reference, modes of mental activity, primary and secondary consciousness, and the distinction between inner perception and inner observation.
