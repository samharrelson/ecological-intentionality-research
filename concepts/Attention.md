description: Notes on attention as an ecological and phenomenological practice.
---
Attention here names more than concentration. It is a practice of allowing a creature or place to appear with increasing determinacy without assuming that greater clarity means greater possession. The recent sits add another discipline: attention should become more corrigible, not merely more confident, while recognizing that corrected perception does not by itself determine adequate care. The Sept. 28 power-line sit adds an epistemic limit with a Husserlian correction: practical-cultural sense can itself be genuinely given, but the contingent history, law, institutions, and politics through which a place acquired its present form cannot be recovered by looking harder alone. The Oct. 2 sit sharpens the handoff: attention can disclose the need for historical inquiry without disclosing the history itself, and a better-formed perceiver is not yet accountable praxis. Oct. 3 makes the evidential restraint concrete at the walnut’s trunk: a visible knot or wound can warrant inquiry without identifying its own cause. **Visible trace is not self-explanatory history.** Oct. 5 applies the same restraint to differentiated leaflets: color, orientation, holes, marks, light, and shadow can be directly available while their developmental and causal histories remain open. **Difference is given before its explanation is.**
