# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this research project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

The project values careful attention, intellectual generosity, thoughtful disagreement, and accountability in how claims are made and revised.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- engaging arguments rather than attacking people;
- offering criticism in good faith;
- distinguishing correction from disagreement;
- acknowledging uncertainty and limits of evidence;
- respecting differences in disciplinary, cultural, religious, and philosophical perspective;
- citing sources responsibly;
- respecting privacy and intellectual-property boundaries;
- being willing to revise one's own claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks or sustained hostility;
- deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor's position;
- publishing private information without permission;
- posting copyrighted material that is not permitted for redistribution;
- deliberately attempting to expose private research materials or local system information;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a scholarly or professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions from project spaces.
